Context Language Models

ML/AI
Rulin Shao, Shannon Zejiang Shen, Junjie Oscar Yin, et al. (Meta Superintelligence Labs, Univ. of Washington) · arXiv:2609.37725 · Sep 29, 2026

Reframes an LLM's context window as an editable file the model can actively rewrite and compress, rather than a passive append-only log — a genuinely different way to think about memory and long-horizon agent state. Built zero-shot on existing models, CLMs beat state-of-the-art context-management strategies by 11.4% accuracy at 21.5% fewer FLOPs on BrowseComp-Plus, and by 5% accuracy at 59% fewer FLOPs on a 12-hour long-horizon benchmark. Backed by a Meta FAIR-released codebase, with authors including Luke Zettlemoyer and Mike Lewis.

Crown Graphs Maximise the Representation Number of Bipartite Graphs

Math
arXiv:2609.35842 · ~Sep 28–30, 2026

Settles a standing conjecture in word-representable graph theory: that crown graphs (complete bipartite graphs minus a perfect matching) have the largest "representation number" among all bipartite graphs of the same order. Proves every bipartite graph on N≥9 vertices has representation number at most ⌈N/4⌉, matching the crown-graph value exactly and closing a gap that a June 2026 paper had only partially resolved — with the hardest remaining cases settled by computer-checked Boolean-satisfiability certificates.
