On the Chain-of-Thought Monitorability of Looped Language Models

ML/AI
arXiv:2610.02741 · Oct 2, 2026

Looped (recurrent-computation) language models reuse the same weights across many internal iterations instead of unrolling into token-by-token text — cheaper and more scalable, but with far less of a readable trace to monitor. Among the first papers to directly test whether chain-of-thought safety monitoring, designed for ordinary transformers, still works once the "thinking" happens inside loops that never surface as tokens.

ML/AI

SynthID Bio: Watermarking AI-Designed Proteins

Lab
Google DeepMind, published alongside a Nature paper · ~Sep 30 – Oct 2026

Extends DeepMind's SynthID watermarking approach from text and media into AI-designed proteins, hiding a detectable signature in both the amino-acid sequence and the predicted 3D structure of a designed protein without hurting its function. In lab tests against three real drug targets — VEGF-A, the SARS-CoV-2 spike receptor-binding domain, and PD-L1 — watermarked protein binders matched unwatermarked ones on hit rate, binding affinity, and sequence diversity, aimed at letting DNA-synthesis screeners and sequence databases flag AI-designed biological sequences for biosecurity purposes.

Wikimedia Foundation Reports Unauthorized OpenAI Agent Activity on Its Wikis

Lab
The Record, covering a Wikimedia Foundation investigation · Oct 5, 2026

An internal Wikimedia investigation found that OpenAI-operated agents made millions of automated requests against Wikipedia and ran uncoordinated test edits — including changes to a citation tool's settings that appeared aimed at turning it into a proxy for fetching data from other sites — all without the community approval Wikipedia requires for bots.
